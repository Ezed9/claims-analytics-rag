# Verizon Total Mobile Protection — Policy Handbook

## 1. Plan Overview

Verizon Total Mobile Protection ("the Plan") covers enrolled mobile devices
against accidental damage, mechanical or electrical failure, and loss or
theft, subject to the deductibles, waiting periods, and exclusions below.
The monthly premium is $17.00 per enrolled line. A maximum of 3 approved
claims per enrolled device is permitted in any rolling 12-month period.

## 2. Waiting Period

### 2.1 Standard Waiting Period

All coverage under the Plan is subject to a **15-day waiting period**
beginning on the enrollment date. Claims filed for incidents that occurred
before the waiting period has elapsed are denied under adjudication code
`DENIED_WAITING_PERIOD`, regardless of incident type.

### 2.2 Waiting Period Exceptions

There are no exceptions to the waiting period for devices enrolled outside
of an initial device-activation window.

## 3. Deductibles by Incident Type

### 3.1 Screen Damage

Cracked, shattered, or non-responsive touch screens are covered with a
deductible of **$29.00** per approved claim.

### 3.2 Liquid Damage

Damage caused by liquid submersion, spills, or humidity exposure is covered
with a deductible of **$99.00** per approved claim.

### 3.3 Lost or Stolen Devices

Devices reported lost or stolen are covered with a deductible of **$199.00**
per approved claim. A police report or carrier-verified loss affidavit is
required for lost/stolen claims.

### 3.4 Battery Malfunction

Battery defects, including abnormal drain or swelling, are covered with a
deductible of **$49.00** per approved claim.

### 3.5 Hardware Malfunction

Non-battery hardware malfunctions (camera, speaker, biometric sensors) are
covered with a deductible of **$49.00** per approved claim.

### 3.6 Other Accidental Damage

All other accidental damage not enumerated above (cracked back glass, bent
charging ports, frame damage) is covered with a deductible of **$99.00**
per approved claim.

## 4. Repair vs. Replacement

The Plan authorizes either a repair or a full device replacement for any
covered incident, at the sole determination of the adjudicating economic
model, except for lost or stolen devices, which are eligible for
replacement only (no repair is possible for a device that cannot be
recovered).

## 5. Exclusions

The following are excluded from coverage under all circumstances:

1. Cosmetic damage that does not impair device function.
2. Damage caused by unauthorized modification or third-party repair.
3. Devices reported as lost or stolen more than 60 days after the incident
   date.
4. Consequential or incidental damages, including data loss.

## 6. Claim Limits

Section 1 caps approved claims at 3 per rolling 12-month period per
enrolled device. A 4th claim in the same period is routed to manual
underwriting review regardless of incident type.

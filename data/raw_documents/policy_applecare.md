# AppleCare+ — Policy Handbook

## 1. Plan Overview

AppleCare+ provides coverage against accidental damage and hardware
malfunction for enrolled Apple devices. Two variants are available:

- **AppleCare+** (base plan, monthly premium $9.99) — does not cover loss
  or theft.
- **AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss** (monthly premium $13.99) — extends
  base coverage to include lost or stolen devices.

Both variants carry **no enrollment waiting period** (0 days); coverage is
effective immediately upon enrollment.

## 2. Deductibles by Incident Type (Both Variants)

### 2.1 Screen Damage

Screen repairs are covered with a deductible of **$29.00** per approved
claim. Screen incidents under AppleCare+ are repair-only; whole-device
replacement is not authorized for screen damage.

### 2.2 Liquid Damage

Liquid damage is covered with a deductible of **$99.00** per approved
claim and may result in repair or replacement depending on the severity of
internal component damage.

### 2.3 Battery Malfunction

Battery issues are covered under the "other damage" deductible tier of
**$99.00** per approved claim, unless the malfunction is determined to be
a manufacturing defect (see Section 2.4).

### 2.4 Hardware Malfunction (Manufacturing Defect)

Hardware malfunctions attributable to a manufacturing defect are covered
at **no deductible** ($0.00), consistent with Apple's standard limited
warranty terms extended under AppleCare+.

### 2.5 Other Accidental Damage

All other accidental damage is covered with a deductible of **$99.00** per
approved claim.

## 3. Lost or Stolen Devices

### 3.1 Base AppleCare+ (No Theft & Loss)

Lost or stolen devices are **not covered** under the base AppleCare+ plan.
Claims filed for lost or stolen devices under this variant are denied
under adjudication code `DENIED_NOT_COVERED`.

### 3.2 AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss

Lost or stolen devices are covered with a deductible of **$99.00** per
approved claim. Replacement is the only available remedy; repair is not
applicable to a device that cannot be recovered.

## 4. Claim Limits

Both AppleCare+ variants permit **unlimited claims** (no maximum annual
claim count), subject to the per-incident deductibles above.

## 5. Exclusions

1. Cosmetic damage that does not impair device function.
2. Damage caused by unauthorized modification or third-party repair not
   performed by an Apple Authorized Service Provider.
3. Intentional damage.

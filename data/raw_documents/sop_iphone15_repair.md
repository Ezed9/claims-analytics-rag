# iPhone 15 Family — Depot Repair Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Scope

This SOP covers authorized depot repair procedures for the iPhone 15
family (iPhone 15, iPhone 15 Plus, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max).
Technicians must confirm the exact model via Settings > General > About
before ordering parts, as part numbers differ across the family even
though the procedures below are shared.

## 2. Screen (Display Assembly) Repair

### 2.1 Symptoms

Cracked glass, unresponsive touch input, discoloration, or spiderweb
fracture patterns on the front display.

### 2.2 Procedure

Remove the display assembly using a heat-based adhesive release, transfer
the True Tone sensor and earpiece speaker to the replacement OLED panel,
and recalibrate True Tone via depot diagnostic software after
reassembly.

### 2.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $129.00
Labor: 45 min

## 3. Battery Replacement

### 3.1 Symptoms

Rapid battery drain, unexpected shutdowns below 30% charge, or a swollen
battery case.

### 3.2 Procedure

Disable battery service warnings via depot diagnostic software after
installation, use adhesive pull-tabs to remove the old cell without
puncturing it, and verify charge cycles reset correctly.

### 3.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $49.00
Labor: 30 min

## 4. Back Glass Replacement

### 4.1 Symptoms

Cracked or shattered rear glass panel, typically from a fall on a hard
surface.

### 4.2 Procedure

Use a laser-assisted back glass removal fixture to avoid damaging the
internal frame, then bond the replacement panel with depot-approved
adhesive film and cure under UV light for 10 minutes.

### 4.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $89.00
Labor: 60 min

## 5. Charging Port Replacement

### 5.1 Symptoms

Bent or unresponsive USB-C port, intermittent charging, or a cable that
will not seat fully.

### 5.2 Procedure

Replace the charging port flex assembly, checking for corrosion on
adjacent board contacts, and run a full charge-cycle diagnostic before
closing the case.

### 5.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $35.00
Labor: 40 min

## 6. Liquid Damage Remediation

### 6.1 Symptoms

Device will not power on, intermittent charging, or a triggered liquid
contact indicator after exposure to water or another liquid.

### 6.2 Procedure

Fully disassemble the device, ultrasonically clean the logic board and
connectors, inspect for corrosion on the battery connector and charging
IC, and replace any corroded components before reassembly and a 24-hour
soak test.

### 6.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $75.00
Labor: 90 min

## 7. Camera Module Replacement

### 7.1 Symptoms

Camera fails to focus, produces blurry images, or the camera app crashes
on launch.

### 7.2 Procedure

Replace the rear camera module, recalibrate optical image stabilization
via depot diagnostic software, and verify focus accuracy across all
lenses before returning the device to the customer.

### 7.3 Parts and Labor

Part cost: $95.00
Labor: 50 min
